・ The graphi's title reveals that the survey question was あなたは（どれくらい）幸せですか。
・ The results indicate that 13% of the people surveyed selected かなり幸せ as their response, and 47%
responded with まあ幸せ、 When these two levels of happiness，かなり and まあ， are combined, we see
that 60% of the respondents feel happy. As this example shows, analyses of survey results sometimes
take multiple groups with different degrees of the same condition and treat them as a single group.
