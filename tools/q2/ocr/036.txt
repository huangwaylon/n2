＜to be in the process of doing）
［読み物 1-行13］

Although the economy has been in a recession for a long time, it has been gradually recovering recently.
国政治に興味を持つ若者が次第に増えつつあるようだ。

国スマホの普及により、家庭の固定電話で話す時代は終わりつつあるようだ。
四年賀状（New Year's card）を送る習慣が日本から消えつつあるのは残念だ。
・～つつある is used with verbs that denote a change, and expresses that

the change is currently in the process of occurring.
・When ～つつある is used with verbs that typically represent gradual changes, it implies that the
change began recently and is about to go through a bigger transition （see and 回）.
・ However, when used with verbs that represent instantaneous changes （e.g， 終わる、消える）， this
expression implies that the transition is about to be completed （see Band 国）.
・ This expression is often accompanied by adverbs expressing the degree or speed of change，
such as次態に、体的に、だんだん、Or少しずつ。
・～つつある is not used with verbs for actions that are currently taking place.
