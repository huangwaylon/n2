データの説明 Explanations of data
Passages that explain certain data often include （A）調査結果（the results of a survey or study），（B）結果か
らわかること（conclusions that can be drawn from the results）， and （C） 筆者や専門家の青餐（意見・考え）
（a discussion of the opinion/interpretation of the writer or an expert）. However, these elements do not
always follow the order of （A），（B） and （C）. Consequently, as you read such a passage, it's a good idea to
keep track of which part is whichー（A），（B） or （C）.
｜ストラテジー」
▶（A）調査結果 presents specific numbers and comparisons of numbers. Expressions often used
include words like 達する、占める、とどまる、多い／少ない、高め／低め、最もand～より。
▶（B）結果からわかること presents what can be said based on the evidence of the results. Expressions
like 〜がわかる，～がうかがえる、～ようだ，and傾向があるare typically used here. Usually, this part
follows the results section, but note that there also cases where the order is reversed.
▶（C）筆者や専門家の考察 does not discuss the survey itself, Instead, it presents the opinion of the
writer or an expert regarding the results, such as making inferences about the causes behind
the results, based on the findings of other studies, social factors, and so on.
