Nなんか can be replaced with N なんて.
例）A：ねえ、昨日の夜9時からのドラマ見た？

なんて and なんか are interchangeable when preceded by a noun， but なんか cannot be used in place
of なんて when it's preceded by a sentence. "X （sentence） なんてY” mentions the sentence as a topic
and expresses the speaker's strong opinion （Y） about the topic. In such use, X typically expresses
the speaker's personal experience or something that the speaker saw or heard, while Y takes
expressions of emotions such as じられない、あり得ない、うらやましいandひどい。
例）パク：あれ？スミスさんじゃないですか？！
Hey! It's you, Smith-san！
スミス：あっ、パクさん！こんなところで会うなんて、言じられない！
Park-san! What a surprise to run into you here！
