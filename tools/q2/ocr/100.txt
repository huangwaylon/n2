タイトルから読み取れるメッセージ Interpreting the writer's message from the title
The key message that a writer seeks to convey in a story, essay, or other such writing is sometimes hidden
in the title, so taking a good look at the title may help you to better understand that message.

・ A title may directly state the writer's message, but this is not always the case. Either way, understanding
the meaning of the title helps you to decipher the message as you work your way through the passage.
・In the case of this example, the title わたしと小鳥とすずと alone does not clearly reveal the poem's
message. So, you need to focus on the keywords わたし、小鳥，and すず and use them as a compass for
identifying the message. The last part of the poem brings together all three words： すずと、小鳥と、それ
からわたし、みんなちがって、みんないい。This can be considered the writer's message.
