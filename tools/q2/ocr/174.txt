11. Nでも
＜for example； （how about you do） such-and-such>
［読み物2-行30］
A：ああ、疲れた•・・・・・。
Ah, I'm tired...
B：甘いものでも食べたら？元気が出るよ。
Why don't you eat something sweet? It'll help you feel better.
日 A：そろそろ出かけようよ。

国A：今度の三津体、久しぶりにどこかに行きたいなあ。

・Nでも mentions one example of ideas, and implies that there are other
N （prt.）でも
options as well.
・ The example mentioned by でも does not significantly change the speaker's main point with or
without it, but N でも can be used to give mild suggestions or directions without a forcible tone.
・The particles を、が、は and も can be replaced byでも，as shown in . Other particles such as で、
にand と，precede でも and take the form "N + particle+でも”（see 図，and国）.
