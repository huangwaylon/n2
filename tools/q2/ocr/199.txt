一口にN といっても
ひとくち
useal to make a statement （Y） that there are many different types of
items even if they are grouped together into one simple category/word.
・ Words such as いろいろ、様々 and 多様 are often used in statement Y.
・一口に can be omitted in this structure, as shown in B.
② XといってもY <Although X,itisonly/justy）
A： 週末、デートなんだって？
You're going on a date this weekend？
B：デートといっても、歩いて 10分の近くの公園に行くだけだよ。
Even though it may be a date, we're just going to a park that's a 10-minute walk away.
