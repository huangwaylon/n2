かえって
・かえって is used to emphasize that the actual result is the opposite of what
was expected by the speaker.
★むしろ（L7-5） and かえって
むしろand かえって are similar in that they both express that something was the opposite of an
expectation.X むしろY compares X and Y, and states that the speaker holds an opinion/preference/
judgment （Y） that is the opposite of what was expected （X） based on commonly held belief or
previously discussed topic.✕かえって Y, on the other hand, states that X is an expected result and Y
is the actual result that is the opposite of X.
例1）外食するほうが家で料理するより 10むしろ／〇かえって？安いことがある。
Eating at a restaurant sometimes can be cheaper than cooking at home.
例2）私の場合、日本語は勉強というより10むしろ／Xかえって！趣味だ。
In my case， （learning） Japanese language is more like a hobby than studying.
例3）ルームメートのために部屋を掃除してあげたらも✕むしろ／〇かえって予怒られた。
I cleaned up my roommate's room for him/her, but he/she actually got mad at me for it.
