appropriate for person N. This structure implies that N has his/her
limitations and flaws, but is achieving the best quality in his/her own capacity.
・ N has to be the speaker, or a person considered to be in a position subordinate to the speaker. A
person of higher status cannot be used in place of N.
・それなり is an idiomatic phrase that acknowledges the flaws and limitations of an item discussed
previously, while also recognizing that the item in discussion met its own standards （despite
the flaws and limitations）. In ④， the speaker is acknowledging that he/she was originally
unmotivated to make a skit due to the task's lack of motivating factors, but in the end he/she
enjoyed it to some extent.
