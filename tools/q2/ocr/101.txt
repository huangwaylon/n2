・くらい can be used in a similar way to ほどintroduced in chapter 6; however， くらい is considered
to be less formal than ほど、死ぬほど is an idiomatic phrase, and can not be replaced by くらい.
死ぬほど would be rephrased as 死にそうなくらい。
