「たとえ」を使った表現 Expressions used in analogies
Writings such as stories and essays sometimes employ analogies to express emotions or describe things
and scenery. You'll find that certain expressions are often used in analogies, including～くらい and （まる
で）～ようだ．

▶ Watch out for expressions like（まるで）〜ようだ、（まるで）〜みたいだ、～ほどand〜くらい， and try
to figure out what the writer is expressing with them （namely, what is being likened to what）.
1（まるで）～ようだ/～みたいだ
（a）私の友達はいつも明るく元気で、まるで太陽みたいだ。

（b）「もしもし、」と女が言った。それはまるで安定の悪いテーブルに薄いグラスをそっと
載せたようなしゃべり方だった。（1風の歌を避け！お点養機）
・（まるで）～ようだ and（まるで）〜みたいだ are used to liken a state, quality, or condition to something
else.
・In （a）， the friend's cheerful disposition is likened to 太陽。
・ In （b）， the way that the woman says もしもし is likened to the situation 安定の悪いテーブルに薄いグ
ラスをそっと載せた。Instead of describing her voice in a matter-of-fact way, the writer expresses it
with a vivid image.

・～ほどand～くらい are used to emphasize the degree of some state is considerable.
・ In （a）， the degree of elation is expressed as 夢ではないかと思う， emphasizing that the person was very
happy.
・In （b）， the degree of the sofa's softness, depth, and roominess is being emphasized as considerable.
