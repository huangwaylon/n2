Vます得る／得ない
・／得る orV得ない states whether there is a possibility for V to
happen.V得るmeans it is possible for the action or event expressed
by V to occur, andV 得ないmeans it is not possible for it to occur.
・ This structure is not used to discuss a possibility that is based on the person's ability or
credentials. In such cases, potential forms are used.
例）✕車の運転をし得ない。→●車が運転できない。
・～得る can be pronounced as ～うるwhen itis in dictionary formi when itis in other forms （～得
ない、～得ます，～得た，etc.），える is the only acceptable pronunciation. However, when the verb
used with 得る is考える、うる is used （see図）.
・～得る is generally used in writing, but あり得る and あり得ない are frequently used in speech, as
in 日.
・ Not every verb can be used with this structure. Examples of acceptable verbs are：あり得る／あり
得ない、起こり得る／起こり得ない、考え得る、予想し得る／予想し得ない、想像し得る／想像し得ない、
理解し得る／理解し得ない。
