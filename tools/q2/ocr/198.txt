隠喩 Metaphor
いんゆ
Two categories of literary analogies are similes and metaphors. The expressions studied in Strategy 14 are
examples of the linking words used for similes. Since similes use words like （まるで）～ようだ、（まるで）
～みたいだ、～ほど，or～くらいto convey the comparison, it is easy to tell that an analogy is being made.
In this section, we will look at metaphors, which do not use such expressions and thus make it harder to
see that an analogy is being presented.
