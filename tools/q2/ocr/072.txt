・Xtakes one of the following： （a） nouns or verbs that express change （e.g.，増加、進化，なる、増える、

two actions occur at the same time.

Vたものだ
・Vたものだ is an expression for stating something that the speaker used to
do but no longer does now; it implies that the speaker feels nostalgic
about the memory.
・ This expression is somewhat monologue-like, and is not typically used in conversations.
