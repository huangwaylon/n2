・なり is used to cite examples of options or possibilities that belong

in the same category. It is implied that the speaker is giving just a
few examples among various other possibilities, without expressing
preference for any in particular.
・ Because なり implies that the speaker does not have strong opinions or preferences, it can
be interpreted as meaning that the speaker is indifferent, and thus may strike the listener as
impolite in some situations. Accordingly， なり is not typically used when speaking to someone
of higher status.
・なり cannot be used to describe established facts, such as things that occurred in the past. It can
be used, however, to enumerate examples for a hypothetical situation different from the actual
past event.
