1.やってみよう Before going through the model dialogue, you do a little role-playing in the
dialogue's context to see how well you can communicate in that situation.
2.聞いてみよう As you listen to the model dialogue, you try to understand its content and
flow.
3．モデル会話 Next, you read a script of the dialogue to check your understanding. フロー
チャート serves as a visual aid for gaining a handle on the overall flow of the dialogue.
4.練習しよう You then practice creating output in your own words, following patterns of
expression drawn from the dialogue.
⑨聞く Practice two types of listening comprehension
聞く is also divided into two parts，聴解 1 and 聴解 2, both of which offer content related to the
readings. 聴解 1 provides listening comprehension practice that requires you to identify specific
information from a lecture, explanation, or other material that you listen to while looking as
figures or charts. 聴解 2 presents conversations in which an international student discusses social
topics with a friend. The subject matter lends well to discussion, so this material can also be used
for discussion after listening comprehension practice.
