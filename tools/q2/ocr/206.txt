・✕なり Ymeans that as soon as action X was done, another action or an
Vるなり
unexpected event （Y） followed. Regardless of whetherY is an action or an
event, the subject of Y is never the speaker.
・ Y only takes verbs in the past tense. The present tense, future tense, command form, or a
request form is not used in Y.
・ Y often takes expressions that describe a specific moment in time. Examples of such
expressions are： ～てきた/～ていった， and the past tense of instantaneous verbs （i.e，， verbs that
refer to actions that are completed instantly and don't continue for a period of time）.
例） • 山田さんは店を出るなり、走り出した。
Yamada started running as soon as he stepped out of the store.
• 山田さんは店を出るなり、走っていった。
Yamada left running as soon as he stepped out of the store.
✕ 山田さんは店を出るなり、走った。
