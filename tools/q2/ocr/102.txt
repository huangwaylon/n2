：＞に違いない
*Nだ
・Xに違いない expresses the speaker's strong belief that is intuitive，
but also based on a proof of some sort.
・ The speaker's confidence level expressed by Xに違いないis
higher than ～だろう。
・ This structure is often used in writing.
読む
君く
身に違いない andはずだ（L5-3）
✕はずだ is an expression used when the speaker's belief is based on common sense or logical
evidence.
例1）今日は日曜日だから、銀行は閉まっているはずだ。
The bank should be closed because it's Sunday today.
Xに違いない is used when the speaker's belief is based on his/her intuition or impression.
例2）ジョージはパーティー好きだから、今日のパーティーにも絶対来るに違いない。
George likes parties, so there's no doubt that he'll come to the party today.
