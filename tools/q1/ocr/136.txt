N （prt.）さえ
・Nさえ emphasizes N as an extreme example.
・Nさえ Xmentions N as an extreme example for X, and implies that
since N is X, other things would be X as well.
・さえ also implies the speaker's surprise, self-deprecation, sarcasm, etc. While も can be used
for similar function （i.e，， to mean even in an emphatic way）， さえ conveys the aforementioned
feelings more than も alone.
