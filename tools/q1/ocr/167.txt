せつぞくし
まず、次に， and other adverbs and conjunctions that express sequential order are often used in the instructions
for various procedures.
ストラテジー
▶ Look for expressions like まず、次に、そして、それから and 最後に， and read the sentences
containing them to understand the steps of a procedure and their sequence.

English Guide
・ The sentence beginning with まず explains the first step of the procedure. The next sentence（たいてい、
画面の上のほうにあります） expounds on that step. The sentence with 次に describes the second step，
and the following sentence gives further details. The expressions of sequence other than まずand 最後に
do not necessarily have to be used in the same order as in the example above.
