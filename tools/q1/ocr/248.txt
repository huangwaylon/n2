2．使役形 Causative
しえきけい
A
強制 Compulsion
きょうせい
・Verbs in the causative form generally express that the action was done under compulsion. The
subject of the sentence is the person who forced someone else to do the action.
回お母さん：野菜を食べなさい！
子ども：（本当は食べたくないけど・・・）はい・・・・・。
→お母さんは子どもに野菜を食べさせました。
＊As shown in D above， when the original verb takes an object followed by the particle を
（e.g； 野菜を）， then the person who was forced to do the action （e.g； 子ども） should take
the particle に、This is a grammatical rule called double を constraint, which limits the use of
particle を to once per verb.
図 先生：立ちなさい！
学生：はい…・・・・。
→先生は学生を立たせました。
国部長：来週の出張に森が行きたがっていますが、どうしましょう？
社長：じゃあ、今回は森に行かせよう。
＊ If the original verb is an intransitive verb, the causee （the person who was forced to do the
action） is followed by the particle を， as in 2. Use of the particle に is not prohibited, as shown
in B, but in this case the sentence has the connotations that the subject （causer） considered
the feeling of the person who does the action （causee）. As such, example Bimplies that社長
is showing consideration for 森's desire to go on the business trip.
B
感情の誘発 Induction
• Causative forms of intransitive verbs expressing emotions （e.g，笑う、浮く、怒る、びっくりする、心
配する、る） do not mean that the emotions were “forced" by the causer, rather, they convey that
the emotions were induced by the causer. In such cases, the causee should take the particle を
日兄は弟を泣かせました。
国親を喜ばせるために、いい会社に就職したい。
© 許可 Permission （便袋形（causative）+てあげる／くれる／もらう）
・Causative verbs followed by てあげる／くれる／もらう express giving or receiving of permission
to do the action.
囙弟
：お兄ちゃんの新しいギター、かっこいいね。ちょっと弾いてみてもいい？
私（兄）：いいよ。
→ 私は弟に（私の）ギターを弾かせてあげました。
＊When the speaker （私） gives someone else permission to do something, thenてあげる is
used with the causative form. In D above， 私 is the person who gives permission， and 弟 is the
person who receives the permission （to play the speaker's guitar）.
