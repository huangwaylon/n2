As in "雨が降るどころかすごくいい天気になった”、the "AどころかB"form expresses a feeling that strongly says
"it's not A （rainy weather）， it's the opposite, B （fine weather）." In addition, as in ”漢字どころかひらがなも読めな
い”， it is also used when you say "it's not A （not able to read kanji）， it's something much more, B （not able to
even read hiragana） " A is often something the listener said or something the speaker has just thought of.
