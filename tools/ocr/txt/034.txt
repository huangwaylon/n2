そうな感じがするので、自室の人には使わないほうがいい。親しい人との会話では、「もんだ」
が使われる。
This expresses the speaker's judgment that anyone would think so or that the speaker thinks it Is absolutely
correct. You should not use this when speaking to a higher ranking person as it can sound bossy. ”もんだ" is
used in conversation with a familiar person.
